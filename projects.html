Academic Planner

A task manager for students. Add your assignments, give them a deadline and a priority, and see what needs attention first. Tasks stay on your device after you close the page.

Key features

  • Tasks grouped by deadline: overdue, today and upcoming
  • Search, filter and edit tasks, with undo after a delete
  • Progress bar, live clock and a list of what is due next
  • Export your tasks as a text file

Built with

HTMLCSSJavaScriptlocalStorage
